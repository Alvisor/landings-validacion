Questions

What access does it need?

A read-only role limited to describing alarms, listing metrics and listing resources. It never reads logs, data or secrets, and it can't change anything.

Doesn't Trusted Advisor or Config do this?

They check resource configuration. Neither verifies that an alarm actually receives data and can fire. That cross-check is the product.

Datadog or Grafana support?

The first version is CloudWatch only. Tell us in the form if you need another stack.

What if the scan finds nothing?

Then you got a free audit confirming your alerting works, and you pay nothing.

When does it launch?

Early teams get their first scan in the coming weeks, in sign-up order.

Can it run in CI?

A GitHub Action that blocks deploys introducing dead alarms is planned. Mention it in the form if you want it.
